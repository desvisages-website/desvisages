# Des Visages

A single page in six sections: home, aleas / discography, biography, gallery, live and contact. Native HTML, CSS and JavaScript; no installation or build step. The repository root is ready for GitHub Pages.

## Preview locally

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

Open `http://localhost:8000/`. Titles use **Apfel Grotezk Mittel (weight 500)** by Luigi Gorlero; body text uses **Aujournuit Variable** by Teo Gaudet, initially at regular width 100, except for biography body copy and Contact secondary text in **Anderson Grotesk Regular (400)** by Stephen French. Apfel and Aujournuit are supplied by the owner from [Collletttivo](https://www.collletttivo.it/typefaces/) and self-hosted with their SIL Open Font Licenses in `assets/fonts/`. Anderson uses its real Oblique for emphasis; its source and the designer's personal/commercial use permission are recorded in [the font notes](assets/fonts/candidates/README.md). The other comparison fonts remain inactive.

Apfel is served as WOFF2, with its original OTF also included. Aujournuit is served from the original variable TTF: the supplied `Aujournuit-VariableVF.woff2` actually renders at a fixed width, whereas the TTF preserves the `wdth` axis (50–200). Keep using the TTF unless a replacement is verified to change width in the browser. The earlier Rebak personal-use demo remains ignored by Git and is never requested by the public site.

## Editing the site

- **Sections:** the six `.section-nav` links and six `main > section` elements in `index.html` correspond one to one. The hero menu links to the same sections. The right-side marks track normal page scrolling. Inactive marks are rounded 18 × 3 px lines; the current section becomes an 18 × 9 px rounded rectangle. Mobile uses 15 × 3 px / 15 × 8 px. Both use 24 px row spacing and translucent marks, with stronger opacity on hover / keyboard focus; link targets remain wider than the marks.
- **Photos:** put optimized WebP files in `assets/images/`, then update `<img>` paths, dimensions, alt text, and entries in the `#gallery-data` JSON block. Biography uses the higher-quality landscape `biography.webp` and stays exactly one screen tall (100svh); do not restore fixed pixel or viewport-width-based minimum heights. The image fits the full viewport width with automatic height and no zoom, so neither lateral edge is lost; its vertical position keeps both faces visible. A bottom fade blends it into the dark background, with white copy over a lower gradient. The photograph already has its own texture; no grain or noise is added. The full-size WebP is served unchanged. BIOGRAPHY is part of the first column, with its letters distributed across that column's width and a small optical shift aligning its B and cap height with the body text. The body uses Anderson Regular with slightly tighter leading, justified paragraphs and left-aligned final lines. Desktop normally has three columns and tablet two. The original five paragraphs remain intact in the HTML. `app.js` typesets their words into equal-height columns, including the heading in column one, while preserving paragraph boundaries and fixed 8px paragraph gaps. Sentences may continue in the next column. It measures candidate layouts in the browser and makes small font-size and heading-spacing adjustments; it never distributes spare height between paragraphs. The native column flow remains a readable fallback, and single-column reading areas keep the original paragraphs with small fixed spacing. On narrow phones and very short windows, the text alone scrolls inside a labelled, keyboard-accessible reading area; the photograph, title and section height remain fixed. The original earlier portrait stays available in the photo gallery. Keep visible copy limited to section titles, functional labels and the biography based on the owner’s supplied text; do not add slogans.
- **Films:** the Gallery cover has a single photo background; Photos opens the collection in a lightbox and Videos opens `#video-dialog`. Add videos to that dialog. Native controls, posters and `preload="none"` keep playback intentional and initial loading light.
- **Discography:** aleas spans exactly two screens on tablets and computers, each with its own scroll-snap point and sizes that follow the window height (checked at 1024 × 768, 1280 × 680 / 800, 1440 × 900, 1920 × 1080 and 900 × 1100). As the second screen rises, `app.js` sets `--mix` from 0 to 1, moving the background from #10120f to the deep teal #0b2322, with a lower glow towards #1d4643 that meets the top of the Biography photograph. Phones keep one continuous flow. The first screen holds the title, a one-line description, the Listen (Bandcamp player dialog), Videos (YouTube playlist) and Bandcamp buttons, the credits list and the cover. The second screen holds About (the owner's text in three columns), Video documentation (three cards for *alea II*, *alea III* and *alea V* that play in `#youtube-dialog`, loading the privacy-enhanced YouTube player only on request and removing it on close) and, beside them, a Discography card for the debut EP. The music dialog loads the existing Bandcamp album IDs only when requested and includes a direct Bandcamp fallback link. Buttons, cards and the EP row share Contact's paper fill and red circle on hover / focus.
- **Concerts:** each year is a `.events-year` group (an `<h3>` year plus an optional note) inside `.events-list`. Add an `.event` article to the matching group, newest first, with `data-date="YYYY-MM-DD"` and a matching `<time datetime>`; create a new group for a new year. The whole row links to the event page through `.event__link`; `.event__kind` names where it goes (Venue, Event page, Instagram) and an optional `.event__recording` pill links a video. Rows share Contact's hover fill and arrow circle. JavaScript separates upcoming and archived dates against Europe/Amsterdam time and orders them with CSS `order` (upcoming forward, archive backward), hiding empty years. A known month or year can use `YYYY-MM` or `YYYY`; do not invent missing date components. Only publish confirmed events and actual ticket / venue links. On desktop and tablet the Live section is exactly one screen tall and the list scrolls inside it, fading at the edges while more dates remain; on phones the list flows with the page under a photo band cropped on the piano. The Live title sits in the dark area above the piano.
- **Contacts:** the owner's red portrait is horizontally centered behind the information at 1.3× scale and raised to 35% of the section height to recover the lower bodies. Its unscaled height is capped at one viewport on mobile. The email and primary link names are slightly smaller, labels use sentence case, and the right block is compacted vertically without changing its width or horizontal placement. It uses a local `#d80108` background matching the most common outer-edge RGB value (216, 1, 8) and subtle edge blending. The hero keeps its own `#b9070a`. Slightly smaller typography and wider space between the desktop information blocks leave more of the portrait visible. Titles, email and link names retain Apfel Mittel; secondary labels, handles, URLs, metadata, copying controls and the return link use Anderson Regular. The duo's email (`desvisages1@gmail.com`) leads the section, with César Vidal named as the person handling bookings; a Copy address button appears when the browser supports copying. Links are grouped in this order: Follow (Instagram `@desvisages`), Websites (Jorge Care, César Vidal) and Listen & watch (YouTube `@desvisages`, then Bandcamp last). All handles and addresses were supplied by the owner; do not guess new ones. Group labels sit beside their links on wide layouts and above them when the column is narrow. Every contact link has an outlined arrow circle; on hover, keyboard focus or tap the link fills with paper colour, its text turns red and the arrow turns to point forward.
- **Design:** colour and typography variables are at the start of `style.css`. The favicon `assets/favicon.svg` is the duo's DV monogram, white on #b9070a; bump its `?v=` query in `index.html` when it changes, since browsers cache icons. Mobile flows remain scrollable, and reduced-motion preferences disable smooth scrolling / animations.

## Source material and limits of the first version

The latest biography upload, `DSC_0278.1.webp.zip`, supplies the landscape `biography.webp`: its 2208 × 1643 px WebP is copied unchanged from the latest upload (1,260,856 bytes), with a 1400 × 1042 px mobile version encoded at quality 98 (478,836 bytes). The supplied photograph's texture is preserved; the previous SVG grain overlay was removed. Its full width remains visible; only the vertical framing is adjusted to keep both faces above the text. The expanded biography was approved by the owner on 1 November 2026: the debut EP was recorded at HEM in Geneva, features three original pieces and was recorded, mixed and mastered by Samuel Albert; it was released in 2025 and also presented at Geneva's Fête de la Musique. The final paragraph identifies aleas as recorded at Dijktheater in Amsterdam in 2026 and ends with that year's European tour. Earlier supplied archives provide `bio.webp` for the gallery portrait, `sección-fotos.jpg` for the single Gallery background, `eventos.png` for concerts, and `cover-aleas.png` for the album artwork. Original images were converted to sRGB when needed. The latest hero upload, `1 (todos bordes color sólido).webp`, replaces the earlier transparent cutout. Its 2500 × 2831 px WebP is copied unchanged, with a 1100 × 1246 px mobile version encoded at quality 94. The page background is `#b9070a`, the most common RGB value along its outer edges; subtle CSS masks blend small compression differences at the photograph's top and sides. The larger portrait is framed below the uppercase title to keep faces clear. The Contact portrait comes from the owner's `IMG_4852.1` light-version upload: the original 3014 × 4516 px WebP is copied unchanged as `contact.webp` (2,434,388 bytes), with a 1200 × 1798 px mobile WebP at quality 96 (130,282 bytes). Source archives remain outside Git; only the web assets are committed.

The red portrait was originally credited to Beatriz Álvarez on the personal site. Per the owner's explicit correction, no photographer credit appears in the site or gallery dialogs; source attribution stays here. The bio is edited from the complete text the owner supplied, retaining the instrumental setup, visual work, influences and release history. The aleas video is a 19.8-second excerpt, encoded for the web at 1280 px. It plays in a dialog without automatic audio.

The archive lists every Des Visages concert published on [jorgecare.com/concerts](https://jorgecare.com/concerts) (checked 23 October 2026), with its titles, venues and links: the six dates of the June 2026 European Tour (Pakt Bern, Café Heloïse Paris, DATA Marseille, SubWaves Amsterdam, The Horse Hospital London, Mayhem Copenhagen) and, in 2025, Fête de la Musique (20 June, with On Press Off) and the EP launch concert (21 February, Électrons libérés), both at the HEM Black Box in Geneva. The Horse Hospital's line-up note comes from the venue page.

No future dates are invented. The empty upcoming view provides a booking link.

The long-scroll structure and side navigation take [Ben van Gelder's site](https://www.benvangelder.com/home/) as a reference. This implementation uses original code and the duo's own texts and materials.

## Source publication and hosting

Preserve `CNAME` (`www.desvisages.net`). GitHub Pages publishes from `main` at the repository root. A successful merge into `main` triggers the `pages build and deployment` workflow. Continue using task branches and pull requests for subsequent changes, then integrate / deploy within the owner's authorization.
