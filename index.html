The duo

An unstable
conversation.

Des Visages is César Vidal and Jorge Care: two Chilean musicians working with saxophone, live electronics and light.

The music takes shape in the room. A prepared saxophone is amplified, processed and set against electronic sound; either player can interrupt, prolong or change the direction of a phrase. A sustained tone becomes a texture. A small gesture unsettles the form.

Lighting and moving images take part in the same process. Sometimes they respond to the sound; sometimes they follow their own logic, introducing another element of uncertainty.

Following a debut EP recorded in Geneva in 2025, the duo developed aleas in Amsterdam and presented the work across Europe in 2026.
